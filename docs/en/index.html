Where do the good lines end up?

It's a library book, so you can't mark it. A photo of the page sinks into a camera roll of thousands. Type it into a notes app and the title and page number rarely make it along. Bookmark Shot keeps the sentence, the book and the page with two gestures: snap, then swipe.

Crooked photo, straight page

After you snap a page, line up the four corners and even a slanted shot is flattened like paper. Sharper letters mean better recognition.

Questions

Are my photos or quotes sent anywhere?

No. Text recognition runs entirely on your device with Apple Vision, and your photos and quotes never go to a server. It works offline too. See the Privacy Policy below for details.

Which languages can it read?

Text recognition is done by iOS on your device. It reads books in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Russian and most Western European languages, and on recent versions of iOS also Thai, Vietnamese, Turkish, Indonesian, Polish, Czech, Dutch and the Nordic languages. Greek can't be recognized yet.

How do I pick just the sentence I want?

After you snap the page, rub a finger over the sentence as if using a highlighter, and only those words are selected. Pinch to zoom for precision. There's also an eraser, Clear All and Whole Page.

The title or author came out wrong.

Cover recognition guesses from the size of the lettering, so very decorative covers can trip it up. You can fix the title and author before saving the book.

The page number is wrong.

It's read from the numbers along the edge of the photo. Just correct it in the page field before saving.

Can I see my quotes on more than one device?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on, your books and quotes sync automatically.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, and choose Bookmark Shot. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, then tap Customize. Today's Quote shows up there.

The camera doesn't work.

Check Settings > Bookmark Shot and make sure camera and photo access are allowed. Taking photos needs a real iPhone.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later.
