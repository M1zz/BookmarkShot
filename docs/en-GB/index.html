Bookmark Shot 📚

The lines you underline in a book, swiped with a finger

Keep the sentence you would have underlined in a paper book, with just one photo. Photograph the cover and a page with a passage you like, and the app remembers which book and which page it came from. Rub a finger over the page like a highlighter and only that sentence is saved.

How it works

📖 Add a book

Photograph the cover and the title and author are filled in for you. If the back-cover barcode is in the shot, the ISBN is read too. You can edit everything before saving.

📐 Pick the page area

Line up the four corners with the page and the photo is flattened like paper, so the text comes out sharper.

🖍️ Underline with a finger

Rub over the sentence you want and only those words are selected. Pinch to zoom for a more precise swipe, and use the eraser or Select All. The page number is found for you, too.

🗂️ Your collection

Find your saved lines again in a library sorted by book, with full-text search and favourites.

✨ Today's Quote

Every day one of your saved lines comes back to you, and you can share it as a card.

Your lines stay on your device

Text recognition happens on your device, and your photos and lines are never sent to the developer’s servers. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync across your devices through your own iCloud account. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
